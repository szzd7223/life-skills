# Grit and Growth mindset

## Question 1 - Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Use your own words.
- Grit is defined as passion and perserverance for long term goals, being consistent for years.
- Grit can be built by having a growth mindset, which is the idea that ability to learn is not fixed and can change with your efforts.

## Question 2 - Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.
- Growth mindset is that fact that you know you can get better. It directly challenges the fixed mindset which says that you are either born with talent or without it.
- People with fixed mindset tend to look on the outcomes or results while people with growth mindset tend to look on the process of getting better.
- The key ingredients to growth are:
  1. Efforts
  2. Challenges
  3. Mistakes
  4. Feedback

## Question 3 - What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?
- Locus of control is the degree to which you believe you have ontrol over your life.
- There is external and internal locus of control. External means outside you when people say that you are gifted. Internal means when you believe that you are hard working or putting efforts.
- If you want to feel motivated, you have to take the mindset that you are in control of your life and you are responsible for your emotions.
- We can try to build the internal locus of control by simply solving small problems in our own lives like cleaning messy room and taking time to appereciate that we did it.
