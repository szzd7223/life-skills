# Learning Skills

## Question 1
Feynman Technique says that in order to learn something, be good at teaching others that same thing and explain it clearly.

## Question 2
I liked the idea of recall. Whenever you get stuck while learning something new, do not just go and see the solution. Instead, just try to figure out what you remember, give yourself some space to fail or try.

## Question 3
* Active mode means you are focusing on one thing and trying to get to a solution which might require new ideas.
* Passive mode means you are thinking freely and letting new ideas come.

Combining both active and passive modes of thinking can help in learning. People like Edison and Dali used to do this.

## Question 4

## Question 5
Here are some of the things I can do going forward to improve my learning process:
* Be okay with not having knowledge at the start and try to think of an approach by myself.
* Not over rely on AI chatbots.
* Go to examples in the docs and recreate them instead of just asking AI to do it.
* Focus when there is time to focus and not think about work when there is time to relax.
