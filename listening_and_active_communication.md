# Listening and Active Communication

## Question 1
### What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening? (Minimum 6 points)
1. Avoid getting distracted by your own thoughts.
2. Try not to interrupt the other person. Let them finish and then respond.
3. Use door openeres to let the other person know that you are actively listening.
4. Shaw that you are listening with body language.
5. Take notes during important conversations.
6. Paraphrase what others have said to ensure you both are on the same page.

## Question 2
### According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening? (Write in your own words, use simple English)
- Listen Attentively: Give the speaker your full attention without interrupting.
- Show empathy: Try to understand the speaker's feelings and perspective.
- Paraphrase: Repeat the speaker's message in yout own words to confirm your understanding.
- Ask clarifying questions: Ask questions when something is unclear.
