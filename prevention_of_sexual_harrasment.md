# Prevention of Sexual Harrasment

## Question 1 - What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?
- Unwelcome Advances and propositions.
- Inappropriate comments.
- Asking too much about personal life and making the person uncomfortable.
- Gender based inappropriate remarks.

## Question 2 - What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?
- Tell the harasser that the abusive behavior is not okay and ask them to stop.
- Keep records of proof such as Date, Time, location, Possible witnesses.
- If the harrasment does not stop, report the incident in writing to ICC member.
- Acknowledge the rights I have as a citizen as well.
