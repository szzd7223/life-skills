- I can use this to build new habits easier by breaking down tasks into smallest habits which requires lower motivation to do it and are easy to do and combine it with prompts. Something like, After I do [ususal thing I do] I will do [new tiny habit].

## Question 6 - Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?
Habits can be made easier by reducing friction and making them convenient to perform. Use the Two-Minute Rule: scale a new habit down to something that takes two minutes or less, making it easy to get started and build consistency.

## Question 7 - Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?
Bad habits can be made harder by increasing friction and adding obstacles between you and the behaviour. Use commitment devices and increase the number of steps required to perform an unwanted habit, making it less convenient.
